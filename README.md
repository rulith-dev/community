# Rulith Community

Questions, ideas and show-and-tell for everything Rulith makes. Join the conversation in
[Discussions](https://github.com/rulith-dev/community/discussions).

## Products

- **[Rulith](https://rulith.ai)**: verifiable execution infrastructure for AI agents. Docs at
  [docs.rulith.ai](https://docs.rulith.ai).
- **[Rulith Local](https://github.com/rulith-dev/rulith-runtime)**: the local runtime of Rulith, in Agent, Worker
  or Agent+Worker mode.
- **[Rulith Inference](https://github.com/rulith-dev/rulith-inference)**: fast local inference for a 125B
  mixture-of-experts model on one AMD Strix Halo machine, on Windows.
- **[Deep Rulith](https://github.com/rulith-dev/deep-rulith)**: DeepSeek Harness with Rulith as its tools.

## Where to post

- **Questions, help, ideas, things you built:** [Discussions](https://github.com/rulith-dev/community/discussions).
- **Bugs:** the issue tracker of the product's own repository.
- **Security issues:** please do not post them publicly. Email contact@rulith.com instead.

When you ask for help, include the product and its version, your hardware and operating system, and the relevant
part of the log. That is usually what an answer needs.

## Ground rules

Be kind and stay on topic. No spam or advertising. Write in English or Chinese; both are welcome.
